A page behind the tab switcher

The switcher opens on the previous tab, as the first press does. Use the arrow keys, hover or click a card, press Escape to close it, and Space to open it again. Add ?theme=dark or ?theme=light to force a color scheme, and ?hue=150 for another Volet color.

Volet keeps a screenshot of the pages you visit, so the switcher can show them. Tabs it hasn't captured yet show their favicon instead.
